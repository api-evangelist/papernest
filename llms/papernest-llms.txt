# Papernest

> Papernest is a French technology company (founded 2015, Paris) that provides a digital platform for managing, switching, and cancelling household subscriptions — energy, telecom/internet, mobile, and home insurance — through a single interface, with a focus on people who are moving home. It operates in France, Spain, Italy, and the UK, is free to end users, and earns commission from supplier partners. Papernest does not publish a public developer API; it operates a private partner integration program (partner sandbox portal) and an official GitHub organization with only archived open-source utilities.
